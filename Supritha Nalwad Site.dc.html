ABOUT
More than just maths tuition. We build mathematical thinkers.
A strong maths student isn't someone who knows every formula. It's someone who understands the concept, knows how to approach a problem, and has the confidence to find the solution. My teaching focuses on developing all three. I work with students to strengthen their foundations, close learning gaps and gradually develop the skills needed to tackle unfamiliar and challenging problems. With personalised 1-to-1 and small-group online classes, I work closely with each student to help them become more confident, independent learners.
