ABOUT
I teach the child in front of me, not the syllabus behind them.
Most children who come to me don't have a maths problem. They have a confidence problem with a maths-shaped hole underneath it — a topic from two years ago that nobody noticed they missed, and every lesson since has been built on top of it. My first job is to find that hole. My second is to fill it without ever making a child feel behind. Only then do we talk about marks, papers and exam technique — and by that point they usually take care of themselves. I teach one-to-one, online, to families in the UK, Australia, New Zealand and the United States. I plan and teach every lesson myself, and after each one you'll get a short written note: what we covered, what landed, and what to practise before next week.

COURSES · UNITED KINGDOM
11+, KS2, KS3 and GCSE maths.
Lessons follow the National Curriculum and your child's exam board, at their pace. Every course below starts the same way: a free assessment, then a written plan you can actually read.

11+ entrance
Years 4–6 · GL, CEM and independent-school papers · 12–18 months ideal lead time
The 11+ rewards children who stay calm and read carefully — not the fastest arithmetic. We build both: the topic knowledge, then the timing strategy that stops good children losing easy marks in the last ten minutes.
WHAT WE COVER
· Multi-step word problems· Fractions, ratio & percentages· Non-verbal & spatial reasoning· Timed past papers with review
KS2 & KS3
Years 3–9 · National Curriculum · SATs support available
This is where most gaps are made and where they're cheapest to fix. We go back far enough to find solid ground, then move forward quickly — usually faster than parents expect, because the child stops guessing.
WHAT WE COVER
· Number sense & place value· Fractions, decimals, ratio· Early algebra & sequences· Geometry, shape & measure
GCSE — all boards
Years 10–11 · AQA, Edexcel, OCR · Foundation & Higher · Retakes welcome
Two years of content, one clear order. We work through topics by weight in the paper, then spend the final term on past papers and mark-scheme reading — because knowing the maths and knowing how it's marked are different skills.
WHAT WE COVER
· Algebra, graphs & functions· Trigonometry & geometry· Probability & statistics· Past papers & exam technique

Common questions
How do lessons actually run?Video call with a shared whiteboard. Your child writes as we go; nothing needs installing beyond a browser.
What about the timezone difference?Slots are booked and shown in your local time. Australian and NZ families usually take early-morning UK hours; US families take UK evenings.
Is there homework?A short practice set each week — around twenty minutes. It's marked and reviewed at the start of the next lesson.
Can parents sit in?Always welcome, especially for the first few lessons. Many parents prefer the written note instead — both work.
Safeguarding?Background-checked, lessons recorded on request, and a parent contact on file for every student.
